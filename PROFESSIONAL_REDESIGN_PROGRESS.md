## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

1. **Colors**: Muted, professional palette
   - Primary actions: `accent-600` (blue)
   - Success: `success-600` (teal)
   - Text: `secondary-700` to `secondary-900`
   - Backgrounds: `white` and `secondary-50`

2. **Typography**: Smaller, readable
   - Base: 14px
   - H1: 2xl (24px)
   - H2: xl (20px)
   - H3: lg (18px)
   - Body: sm (14px)
   - Small: xs (12px)

3. **Spacing**: Tighter, professional
   - Reduced padding and margins
   - Consistent spacing scale

4. **Components**: Clean, minimal
   - Subtle borders instead of heavy shadows
   - Rounded corners (not rounded-lg)
   - Professional button styling
